UPPER BODY WORKOUT

Build strength, improve endurance and train your upper body with this simple workout plan.

🏋

PUSH-UPS

Keep your body straight and lower your chest close to the floor.

3 Sets × 12-15 Reps
🏋♀

BENCH PRESS

Lower the bar slowly and push it upward while keeping proper control.

3 Sets × 8-12 Reps
💪

SHOULDER PRESS

Press the weights upward and return slowly to the starting position.

3 Sets × 10-12 Reps
🏋

BICEP CURL

Keep your elbows close to your body and curl the weights upward.

3 Sets × 10-12 Reps
🙆

PULL-UPS

Pull your body upward while keeping your grip firm and controlled.

3 Sets × 8-12 Reps
🏋♂

BENT-OVER ROW

Bend slightly forward and pull the weight toward your waist.

3 Sets × 10-12 Reps
💪

TRICEP DIPS

Lower your body using your arms and push back to the starting position.

3 Sets × 10-12 Reps
🔥

LATERAL RAISES

Raise the dumbbells sideways until your arms reach shoulder level.

3 Sets × 12-15 Reps
